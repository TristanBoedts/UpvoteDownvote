Upvote Drift Playground

Candidates (or posts) compete for votes along a spectrum. Each person votes for the nearest candidate. Everyone is free to move. Where do they end up? Drag candidates, press play, and change the rules.

Rules

Speculative. This tab assumes that whoever wins drags the crowd's sense of the centre toward themselves. That is an assumption, not a finding. The direction of the slide is chance.
0 = the crowd's sense of the centre never moves (this is tab 2). Higher = each round it slides a bit further toward whoever is winning.
0 = from the start.
A teaching toy based on the work in Free to Provoke (Burnat & Boedts). Simplified on purpose: it shows the mechanism, not a forecast. Real systems also have strategic voting, uneven downvotes and people who react to what they dislike. See the README.
